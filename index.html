About Me

Background, Web3 journey, expertise, and core principles

I am Mohamed Mostafa (MOHMOS), a 20 year old Computer Science student with a strong passion for Discord communities, blockchain technology, and the Web3 ecosystem.

My Web3 journey began in 2022 with Bag Guild, where I helped build and manage the community. I later contributed to OnChain before joining the LitVM ecosystem as a Protocol Lead. Today, I continue supporting the community through Lit Clinic, helping bridge technical concepts between the product, the team, and the community while improving communication and user experience.

My expertise focuses on building and managing professional Discord servers, organizing roles and permissions, configuring bots, improving community operations, and supporting blockchain communities with scalable and well structured server infrastructure.

Alongside my practical experience, I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science while expanding my knowledge of blockchain technology, decentralized systems, and Web3 development. My long term goal is to become a Blockchain Developer by combining academic knowledge with real world experience gained from Web3 projects.

I believe that honesty, responsibility, and continuous learning are the foundation of professional growth. If a task is outside my current expertise, I communicate that openly instead of making promises I cannot keep. I enjoy learning new technologies, solving challenging problems, working effectively under pressure, and building practical solutions that create value for both teams and communities.
