01About me

About Me

Passionate about Discord communities and the Web3 ecosystem. I specialize in building and managing professional Discord servers, improving community operations, configuring bots, organizing permissions, and supporting blockchain communities.

My Web3 journey started in 2022 with Bag Guild, where I helped build and manage the community. Later I contributed to OnChain before joining the LitVM ecosystem as Protocol Lead, where I continue supporting the community through Lit Clinic.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science while expanding my knowledge of blockchain technology, smart contracts, decentralized systems, and Web3 development. My goal is to become a Blockchain Developer by combining academic learning with practical experience in real Web3 projects.

I value honesty, responsibility, and continuous learning. If I encounter a task outside my current experience, I communicate it honestly instead of making promises I cannot keep. I enjoy learning new technologies, solving problems, working under pressure, and finding practical solutions that improve the workflow for myself and the team.
